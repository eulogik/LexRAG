GST Compliance Calendar — Returns and Due Dates (India, CGST Act 2017)

GSTR-1 — Statement of Outward Supplies (Section 37, CGST Act 2017).
Every registered person must furnish details of outward supplies in FORM GSTR-1.
Due date for monthly filers: the 11th day of the month succeeding the tax period.
Example: GSTR-1 for January is due on 11th February. Do not confuse with GSTR-3B.

GSTR-3B — Summary Return with Tax Payment (Section 39, CGST Act 2017).
Every registered person must furnish a summary return in FORM GSTR-3B and pay
the tax due. Due date for monthly filers: the 20th day of the month succeeding
the tax period. Example: GSTR-3B for January is due on 20th February.
GSTR-3B (20th) is later than GSTR-1 (11th) for the same tax period.

GSTR-9 — Annual Return (Section 44, CGST Act 2017).
Registered persons with aggregate turnover above the notified threshold must
furnish an annual return in FORM GSTR-9. Due date: 31st December following
the end of the financial year. Example: GSTR-9 for FY 2024-25 is due on
31st December 2025.

GSTR-9C — Reconciliation Statement (Section 44, CGST Act 2017).
Registered persons above the notified turnover threshold must furnish a
self-certified reconciliation statement in FORM GSTR-9C along with the
annual return. Due date: 31st December following the end of the financial
year, same as GSTR-9.

CMP-08 — Composition Levy Quarterly Statement (Section 10 read with Rule 62).
Taxpayers under the composition scheme furnish FORM GST CMP-08 quarterly.
Due date: 18th day of the month succeeding the quarter.

Interest on Delayed Payment (Section 50, CGST Act 2017).
A registered person who fails to pay tax by the due date is liable to pay
interest at 18 percent per annum on the outstanding tax, for the period of
delay. Interest at 18% p.a. also applies to wrongly availed and utilised
input tax credit.
